# StarCatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

StarCatch is an observation tool that calculates the positions of artificial orbital objects on the device. We value your privacy and design the app around data minimization.

## How device information is used

- Location: The app requests precise location by default because low-Earth-orbit objects can show significant parallax as the observer's position changes. Coordinates are used only on device to calculate the observer position and true-north reference. They are not uploaded, shared, or persisted. You may still choose approximate location; StarCatch clearly reports reduced accuracy.
- Device attitude: Gyroscope and motion data are used only to determine where the device is pointing. They are neither saved nor transmitted.
- Local records: Observation history, manual progress, and display preferences are stored only on your device.

If location access is denied, StarCatch uses Beijing as an assumed coordinate and explicitly marks the interface as `OBSERVER: ASSUMED`. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

## Data collection and third parties

The current version creates no user account, contains no advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking code, and sends no personal data to the developer or third parties. SatelliteKit performs orbital calculations entirely on device.

## Retention and deletion

Observation history and preferences remain on device until you clear the observation history, reset related settings, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

## Children's privacy

The app does not intentionally collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Policy updates

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy, its effective date, and the App Store privacy disclosures.
