# Accessibility declaration and verification checklist

Safe to describe after release-candidate verification:

- Reduce Motion: respects both the system setting and StarCatch's motion-suppression preference.
- Information is not distinguished by color alone; states include labels, geometry, outlines, or selection marks.
- Primary top, bottom, and back controls use at least a 44-point hit area.

Verify VoiceOver order and Canvas descriptions, maximum Dynamic Type, and functional contrast on physical devices before making broader Product Page claims.
