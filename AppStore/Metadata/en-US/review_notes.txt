StarCatch has no login, subscription, in-app purchase, advertising, analytics, or backend account. Its core catalog ships in the app, so review does not depend on network access.

Suggested review path:
1. Read or skip the first-run field manual and enter the sky.
2. Allow location and motion access, then move the device. In Simulator, drag the sky to simulate azimuth and elevation.
3. Bring the central reticle near a bright orbital object to observe sensing, focusing, locking, and the bottom summary. The summary has a reading grace period and can be pinned.
4. Pinch out from the main sky past the resistance threshold to enter the 3D global view. The globe supports drag rotation, two-finger zoom/rotation, and double-tap reset.
5. Open Settings from the lower-right control to inspect observations, snapshot age, permissions, privacy, and the manual.

When location is denied, StarCatch remains usable with a clearly labeled assumed position. Simulator and degraded pointing modes still demonstrate the complete capture flow.

The app is for education and observation only, not navigation, collision avoidance, or safety decisions.
