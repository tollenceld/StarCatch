StarCatch has no login, subscription, in-app purchase, advertising, analytics, or backend account. Its core catalog ships in the app, so review does not depend on network access.

Suggested review path:
1. Read or skip the first-run field manual and enter the sky.
2. Allow location and motion access, then move the device. In Simulator, drag the sky to simulate azimuth and elevation.
3. Hold the central reticle on an orbital object for about 1.8 seconds. StarCatch locks it automatically, shows a persistent information panel, and writes one local observation. Moving the reticle away does not dismiss the panel; use its upper-right close button or open the detailed archive.
4. Tap Global in the bottom bar, or pinch the sky smaller past the threshold, to enter the 3D global view. Drag to rotate and pinch to zoom; use the shared upper-left return control to return to the sky.
5. Open Records from the bottom bar to review or delete observations. Settings contains snapshot age, permissions, privacy, and the manual.

When location is denied, StarCatch remains usable with a clearly labeled assumed position in Shanghai. Simulator and degraded pointing modes still demonstrate the complete capture flow.

The app is for education and observation only, not navigation, collision avoidance, or safety decisions.
